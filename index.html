Digital Repository of Schlotheimophyllum patellatum (Schlotheim, 1820)

Tomasz Wrzołek & Michał Zatoń

Welcome to the digital paleontological repository of the rugose coral species Schlotheimophyllum patellatum from the Silurian of Gotland, Sweden.

This repository provides open access to 225 high-resolution scans of thin sections and acetate peels, which formed the comprehensive dataset for the publication: Wrzołek & Zatoń 2023 (doi.org/10.14241/asgp.2023.09).

All specimens, their images and metadata are housed by the Institute of Earth Sciences, University of Silesia in Katowice, Poland, and are released here under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0).
